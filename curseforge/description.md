# WarlockGod

*Quality of life for warlocks in WoW Forever · Mejoras para brujos en WoW Forever*

### At a glance

- 🔥 **Your procs, small and pretty**: shadow flames on Shadow Trance, souls rising on Soul Harvest, a ring of fel fire on Decimation and an ember over each shoulder for Shadow and Flame. A burst when it procs, calmer while it lasts, with its seconds. Only the ones your build's talents give
- 👿 **Your demon**: a reminder when you have none out (one click summons it), Soul Link when it isn't on, and HEAL YOUR DEMON! when its health drops
- 🧾 **Clickable reminders**: Demon Skin or Demon Armor, your healthstone and soulstone (make them, and use the soulstone when it's ready), the Firestone or Spellstone that Forever puts on your weapon, and Find Herbs or Find Minerals out in the world. Click one to cast it
- 🎯 **Your effects on your target**: Corruption, Immolate, your Bane and Curse, Siphon Life, Fear... small icons with their seconds beside its nameplate
- ⏳ **Your big cooldowns back**: Death Coil, Howl of Terror, Fel Domination, Inferno, your Soulstone... their icon big in the middle when ready again
- ✋ **INTERRUPT!** when your target casts and your Felhunter's Spell Lock is ready
- 🛡️ **Shield bar**: what's left of your Shadow Ward, your Voidwalker's Sacrifice, a protection potion or any other shield on you
- 🪄 **Wand swing timer**: a bar for your Shoot, dim until the shot comes, with a flash and its seconds
- 💎 **Soul shard count**: red when you're short, orange when you carry too many
- 🍖 **Beside the chat**: Well Fed (+5% XP from kills in Forever) with your XP food at a click, the reagents you're running low on, worn gear and full bags
- 📏 **Range to your target**: melee, drain range, in range of your main spell, at the edge of it or out of range, on the target's nameplate
- ⚠️ **Aggro warning**, before you pull it off your demon or the tank
- 💰 **Talent reset price**: at your trainer, when the price of resetting your talents drops

### De un vistazo

- 🔥 **Tus procs, pequeños y bonitos**: llamas de sombra con Trance de las Sombras, almas que suben con Cosecha de almas, un anillo de fuego vil con Diezmar y una brasa sobre cada hombro con Sombra y llama. Un estallido al salir, más tranquilo mientras dura, con sus segundos. Solo los que dan los talentos de tu build
- 👿 **Tu demonio**: un aviso cuando no tienes ninguno fuera (con un clic lo invocas), Enlace de alma cuando no está puesto, y ¡CURA A TU DEMONIO! cuando le baja la vida
- 🧾 **Avisos clicables**: Piel o Armadura demoníaca, la piedra de salud y la de alma (hacerlas, y usar la de alma cuando está lista), la piedra de fuego o de hechizo que Forever pone en el arma, y Buscar hierbas o Buscar minerales fuera de mazmorras. Con un clic se lanza
- 🎯 **Tus efectos en el objetivo**: Corrupción, Inmolar, tu Bane y tu maldición, Succionar vida, Miedo... iconos pequeños con sus segundos junto a su placa
- ⏳ **Tus cooldowns gordos de vuelta**: Espiral de la muerte, Aullido de terror, Dominación vil, Inferno, tu Piedra de alma... su icono en grande en el centro cuando vuelven
- ✋ **¡INTERRUMPE!** cuando tu objetivo lanza algo y el Bloqueo de hechizo de tu manáfago está listo
- 🛡️ **Barra de escudos**: lo que le queda a tu Custodia de las Sombras, al Sacrificio del abisario, a una poción de protección o a cualquier otro escudo que te pongan
- 🪄 **Barra de la varita**: una barra para tu Disparar, apagada hasta que llega el disparo, con un destello y sus segundos
- 💎 **Contador de fragmentos de alma**: en rojo cuando te faltan, en naranja cuando llevas demasiados
- 🍖 **Junto al chat**: Bien alimentado (+5% de experiencia por muerte en Forever) con tu comida de exp a un clic, los reactivos que te faltan, el equipo gastado y las bolsas llenas
- 📏 **Distancia al objetivo**: cuerpo a cuerpo, alcance de drenar, a tiro de tu hechizo principal, al límite o fuera de alcance, en la placa del objetivo
- ⚠️ **Aviso de agro**, antes de quitárselo a tu demonio o al tanque
- 💰 **Precio de reiniciar talentos**: en tu instructor, cuándo baja el precio de reiniciar los talentos

Everything is set up from the **minimap button** or `/warlockgod`. English and Spanish.
Todo se configura desde el **botón del minimapa** o con `/brujo`. En inglés y en español.

## In combat too

Forever hides your buffs, your target's debuffs, your cooldowns, your shields and your demon's health from addons during a fight. WarlockGod has the game itself draw them: the procs and their countdowns, your effects on the target, the cooldowns coming back, the shield bar and the demon's health warning all work in combat.

## In detail

- **Procs**: Shadow Trance (Affliction, Nightfall), Soul Harvest (Affliction), Decimation (Demonology) and Shadow and Flame (Destruction: its Shadow half after Conflagrate, its Flame half after Shadowburn). Each one only for a build with its talent. Each with its switch and its colour; their size, position and strength in the Procs tab. `/warlockgod procs` shows them and says which your build lacks.
- **Demon**: the reminder summons the last demon you summoned, or the one you choose. Not while mounted or dead, nor while Demonic Sacrifice's buff is on you (unless you have Demonic Pact). The health warning shows under the line you pick.
- **Reminders**: a row of icons above your character, each in a thin black frame: grey when something is missing, in colour with its time left in its last minutes. Out of combat, unless you want them in fights too. A stone is only suggested when you have a soul shard to make it; the Firestone and the Spellstone go on the weapon in your main hand.
- **Your effects on the target**: only yours, and only of the spells you know: Corruption, Immolate, your Bane, your Curse, Siphon Life, Fear, Banish, Howl of Terror and Death Coil, and with their talents Shadow Vulnerability and Demonic Brand. Each group can be switched off; their size is yours.
- **Cooldowns back**: Death Coil, Howl of Terror, Fel Domination, Amplify Curse, Inferno, Ritual of Doom and your Soulstone's half hour; Soul Fire, Bane of Doom and Shadow Ward too if you want them. Only the spells you know, never for the global cooldown; two at once come one after the other.
- **INTERRUPT!**: beside your target while it casts something you can stop, with a Felhunter out that knows Spell Lock and Spell Lock ready. Its reach is the Felhunter's, which the addon can't measure.
- **Shield bar**: as wide and tall as you want, flat, with a one-pixel black edge and the shield's icon on its left. Each shield in its colour, the potions in their school's.
- **Wand**: Shoot's swing timer, as HunterGod's Auto Shot: it shows while you shoot and a moment after, grey when your target is out of reach.
- **Beside the chat**: a click on the food reminder lists your XP food. Reagents: Infernal Stone and Demonic Figurine (and Soul Shards if you switch the shard count off), not the vendor ones with Reagent Economy; a vendor that sells one you lack says so. Gear under 25% and two free bag slots or fewer, both yours to change; a vendor that repairs tells you how worn your gear is.
- **Range**: red in melee (5 yd), yellow in drain range (Drain Life, 20 yd, or more with Grim Reach), green in range of your main spell and lilac in its last 5 yards. Your main spell is the last one you cast of Shadow Bolt, Immolate and Corruption, or the one you choose. In yards or metres.
- **Aggro**: AGGRO 92% before you take it off your demon or the tank, and YOU HAVE AGGRO! when you do.
- **At your trainer**: a note under its window says when you last reset your talents, what it costs now, and when the price drops and to what. The game doesn't tell addons, so WarlockGod writes down each reset you pay for and counts from there, as players report it. The price the trainer asks always wins.
- Drag any display where you want it with **Move displays** (or `/warlockgod unlock`).

## Commands

- `/warlockgod` (or `/brujo`): the options
- `/warlockgod unlock` · `lock`: move the displays
- `/warlockgod procs`: try the procs and see which ones your build has
- `/warlockgod cooldowns` · `pulso`: try the pulse of a cooldown back
- `/warlockgod wand` · `varita`: try the wand's swing timer
- `/warlockgod buffs` · `bufos`: what the addon sees of your armor, stones and shards
- `/warlockgod range` · `rango`: what each range check says about your target
- `/warlockgod respec` · `talentos`: when the talent reset price drops
- `/warlockgod meters` · `yards`: the range in metres or yards
- `/warlockgod button` · `boton`: show or hide the minimap button
